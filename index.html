PET Plastic Bottle

Material: Polyethylene Terephthalate (#1)

🗑️ Dispose in: Blue Recycling Bin

Preparation & Disposal Steps

  1. Empty all liquid and rinse bottle clean.
  2. Leave cap securely tightened or separate per local rule.
  3. Crush bottle flat to conserve collection volume.

Creative Reuse / Upcycle Idea

Cut the bottom half to create a mini herb planter or self-watering seedling pot.

Eco Impact

Recycling saves ~70% energy compared to producing new virgin plastic and keeps ocean ecosystems clean.
